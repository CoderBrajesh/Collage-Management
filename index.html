Who We Are

A Legacy of Education Since 1944

Gaya College, Gaya is one of the oldest and most prestigious institutions in Bihar. Established in 1944, it is a constituent unit of Magadh University, Bodh-Gaya, and is accredited with a 'B' Grade by NAAC.

We offer a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines, fostering an environment of academic excellence and holistic development.

Department in Focus

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

A 2-Year Full-Time Post Graduate Course accredited by AICTE and affiliated to Magadh University.

Prof. (Dr.) Amit Kumar

Head of Department (MCA)

"We are committed to grooming technical experts who can lead the digital future. Join us to excel in Computer Applications."

Course Overview

The MCA program at Gaya College is designed to equip students with advanced skills in software development, programming, and computer applications. Our curriculum bridges the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements.

Eligibility

BCA / B.Sc / B.Com / B.A with Mathematics at 10+2 or Graduation. Min 50% marks (45% for reserved).

Course Fee

Approx ₹90,000 (Total Course Fee) / ₹23,500 per semester.
