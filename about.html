Our Heritage

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Gaya College, Gaya, established in 8th February 1944, is a premier institution imparting holistic education to students from diverse backgrounds.

It was born out of the vision of eminent personalities and educationists of the region who felt the dire need for a center of higher learning in Magadh. Initially affiliated to Patna University, it became a constituent unit of Magadh University, Bodh-Gaya in 1962.

Sprawling over a lush green campus of 57 acres, the college has grown into a multi-faculty co-educational institution offering courses in Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, and Computer Applications. The college is accredited with a 'B' Grade by NAAC.

Our Mission

To provide quality higher education to the youth of this region, particularly those belonging to the underprivileged sections of society, and to empower them with knowledge, skills, and values to face the challenges of the modern world.

Our Vision

To emerge as a center of excellence in higher education, fostering research, innovation, and holistic development of students, thereby contributing to nation-building and social transformation.
